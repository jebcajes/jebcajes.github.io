Research & Publications

Rainfall Forecasting using Support Vector Regression Machines

I served as a co-author of a peer-reviewed research publication featured in the International Journal of Advanced Computer Science and Applications (IJACSA). The research focused on applying machine learning techniques to improve rainfall forecasting and support disaster preparedness initiatives.


My contributions included data preparation, model evaluation, statistical analysis, parameter optimization, forecasting validation, and interpretation of research findings.


The study explored the use of Support Vector Regression Machines (SVRM) to predict rainfall patterns using historical meteorological data collected from Southern Philippines.
